##### Abstract

We study how deposit insurance fragmentation affects the transmission of monetary policy across the euro area. Using ECB loan-level microdata, we show that fragmentation in deposit insurance regimes leads to a 23.7% reduction in policy effectiveness during stress periods, with an associated output loss of 1.2%. We develop an open-economy model with heterogeneous financial intermediaries to rationalise these findings.
