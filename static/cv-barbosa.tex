\vspace{-0.6cm}

\noindent
[Work in Progress] \textbf{Banking Fragmentation and Monetary Policy Transmission in a Currency Union}, \\with Rustam Jamilov and Alexander Haas
\vspace{-0.2cm}
\begin{itemize}
	\item We study how fragmentation in European deposit insurance architectures affects credit allocation and monetary policy transmission, leveraging granular loan-level data from the ECB.
	\vspace{-0.2cm}
	\item We develop an open-economy heterogeneous financial intermediaries model to analyse spillovers from sovereign-debt exposure via bank balance sheets.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{-0.1cm}

\noindent
  [Work in Progress] \textbf{Green Transition in Housing Markets and Optimal Policy}
  \vspace{-0.2cm}
  \begin{itemize}
   \item I study heterogeneous returns to energy-saving technologies across locations and property types in the UK using administrative data from Energy Performance Certificates, the National Energy Efficiency Data framework, and the ECO programme.
   \vspace{-0.2cm}
   \item I develop a quantitative spatial equilibrium model to derive optimal subsidy design for the green transition in housing markets.
  \end{itemize}
